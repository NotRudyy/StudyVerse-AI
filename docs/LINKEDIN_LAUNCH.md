# LinkedIn launch draft

I built **StudyVerse AI — Learn, Ask, Grow**, a study platform designed around one idea: AI should help you learn, not just hand you an answer.

The study loop is simple: bring in your notes, build a StudyPack, learn with Nova, practice, understand your mistakes, and get a plan for what to work on next.

The demo includes a document upload flow, Nova’s Learn/Hint/Socratic/Exam modes, confidence-aware quizzes, Mistake Lab, a skill tree, flashcards, focus sessions, and a weekly Study Wrapped. Seeded demo mode means you can explore without paid AI keys.

**Built with:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, FastAPI, PostgreSQL + pgvector, Redis, Celery, and Gemini/GCS integration points.
