# StudyVerse AI: First-Session Walkthrough

## 01. START HERE - Your first study session

Welcome to StudyVerse AI. This tour shows what each tab does and how the tools fit into one study session. Sample course data is ready, so you can follow along.

## 02. OVERVIEW - Your home base

Overview is your home base. Today's mission, exam countdown, streak, mastery, XP and focus time are here. Start Mission opens the next recommended task. Scroll down for weak topics, packs and quests.

## 03. STUDYPACKS - Organize course material

StudyPacks is your course shelf. Choose Add material or Choose a file to import a PDF, presentation, Word document or image. Open a pack to study from it. In demo mode, uploads stay in the browser; sample packs are ready now.

## 04. NOVA TUTOR - Learn with Nova

Nova Tutor helps you learn from a selected StudyPack. Ask a question, then choose Learn for an explanation, Hint for a nudge, Socratic for guiding questions, or Exam for concise test-style help. Citations point back to your notes.

## 05. PRACTICE - Practice with confidence

Practice asks one question at a time. Choose an answer, rate confidence from one to five, then check it. Nova uses confidence as well as correctness to update your mastery and spot possible misconceptions.

## 06. BOSS BATTLE - Take a full challenge

For a timed challenge, select Boss battle at the top of Practice. It starts the Graph Guardian assessment with ten questions and no hints. Use it after reviewing a topic.

## 07. FLASHCARDS - Recall, reveal, repeat

Flashcards use active recall. Try to answer before you reveal the back, then rate it Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Hard cards return sooner; easy cards wait longer.

## 08. MISTAKE LAB - Fix the idea behind a miss

Mistake Lab gathers your missed concepts, including answers marked with high confidence. Review the explanation, then mark an item fixed when you can explain the correction.

## 09. SKILL TREE - See how topics connect

Skill tree maps topics and prerequisites. Find a lower mastery node, then follow its connections to see what to study first.

## 10. STUDY PLANNER - Choose your next block

Study planner turns your priorities into today's blocks. Complete a block when it's done, and use the remaining list to pick a next step.

## 11. FOCUS ROOM - Start a Pomodoro

Focus room is your Pomodoro timer. Choose a duration, start the timer, and work on one topic. Completing a session records focus time and earns XP.

## 12. STUDY WRAPPED - Look back, then level up

Study Wrapped is your weekly recap. Review active days, focus time, XP and top concepts, then choose one area to keep building next week.

## 13. STUDY ROOMS - Browse shared study rooms

Study rooms is the demo directory for shared study. Browse a room and join to see the flow. This demo shows room listings; live chat and presence are not part of it yet.

## 14. MY PROFILE - Personalize your space

Your profile shows your level, XP, badges and learning preferences. Change the accent theme here. In demo mode, progress stays in this browser session.

## 15. THE STUDY LOOP - One small step at a time

A simple first session is: open a StudyPack, learn with Nova, practice, fix a mistake, then focus and review flashcards. Return to Overview for your next mission. Let's get started.
